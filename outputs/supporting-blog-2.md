## H2: What Is Considered Low Mileage in a Car?

Low mileage refers to a noticeable reduction in your vehicle's fuel efficiency compared to its normal performance.

For example, if your car typically delivers 16 km/l and suddenly drops to 12 km/l without any major changes in driving habits, there may be an issue that requires attention.

Some warning signs include:

* Frequent visits to the fuel station
* Higher monthly fuel expenses
* Reduced kilometers per litre
* Poor engine performance

Monitoring mileage regularly allows you to detect problems early and take corrective action.

## H2: Common Causes of Low Fuel Mileage

Several factors can affect your vehicle's fuel efficiency. Here are the most common causes.

### H2: Dirty Fuel Injectors

Fuel injectors deliver fuel into the engine in a fine spray pattern. Over time, carbon deposits and contaminants build up inside the injectors, restricting fuel flow.

When injectors become clogged:

* Fuel delivery becomes uneven
* Combustion efficiency decreases
* The engine consumes more fuel
* Acceleration performance declines

This is one of the most common reasons for poor mileage in modern vehicles.

---

## H2: Clogged Air Filter

The engine requires a precise mixture of air and fuel for efficient combustion.

A dirty or clogged air filter restricts airflow, forcing the engine to burn more fuel than necessary.

Signs of a clogged air filter include:

* Reduced mileage
* Sluggish acceleration
* Dark exhaust smoke
* Poor engine performance

Replacing the air filter at recommended intervals can significantly improve fuel economy.

---

## H2: Incorrect Tire Pressure

Underinflated tires create additional rolling resistance, causing the engine to work harder.

Even slightly low tire pressure can negatively affect mileage.

To improve fuel efficiency:

* Check tire pressure regularly.
* Inflate tires according to manufacturer specifications.
* Inspect tires for uneven wear.

Maintaining proper tire pressure is one of the simplest **low mileage car solutions** available.

---

## H2: Poor Driving Habits

Driving behavior has a major impact on fuel consumption.

Habits that reduce fuel efficiency include:

* Rapid acceleration
* Hard braking
* Excessive idling
* Overspeeding
* Frequent stop-and-go driving

Adopting smoother driving techniques can help improve mileage significantly.

## H2: Engine Problems and Lack of Maintenance

Skipping routine maintenance can gradually reduce fuel efficiency.

Common maintenance-related issues include:

* Worn spark plugs
* Dirty throttle body
* Faulty oxygen sensors
* Engine misfires
* Poor ignition performance

Regular servicing ensures all engine components operate efficiently.
