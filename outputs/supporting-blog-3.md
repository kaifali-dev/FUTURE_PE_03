## H2: What Is Preventive Vehicle Maintenance?

**Preventive Vehicle Maintenance** refers to routine inspections, servicing, and replacement of wear-and-tear components before failures occur.

Rather than waiting for a breakdown, preventive maintenance focuses on maintaining the vehicle in optimal condition throughout its lifespan.

Typical preventive maintenance services include:

* Engine oil and filter changes
* Fuel injection cleaning
* Brake inspections
* Battery testing
* Air filter replacement
* Tire rotation and balancing
* Coolant system checks
* Engine diagnostics
* Suspension inspections

Regular maintenance helps ensure that every major system in your vehicle functions efficiently and reliably.

## H2: How Preventive Vehicle Maintenance Saves Money

### H2: Prevents Expensive Major Repairs

One of the biggest advantages of preventive maintenance is avoiding costly repairs.

For example:

* Replacing worn brake pads on time is far less expensive than replacing damaged brake discs.
* Changing engine oil regularly costs much less than repairing an engine damaged by poor lubrication.
* Cleaning fuel injectors early can prevent expensive fuel system repairs.

Minor maintenance costs are typically small compared to major component replacements.
