# Prompt Analysis

## Objective

The goal of this prompt is to generate SEO-optimized blog content and a content cluster for an automobile maintenance business.

## Why the Primary Keyword Was Included

The primary keyword helps the AI optimize the blog for relevant Google searches and improve search visibility.

## Why Local SEO Was Included

Including the business location (Vizag) helps target customers searching for automobile services in the local area.

## Why Search Intent Was Included

Search intent ensures that the generated content answers the questions users are actually asking while guiding them toward the business's services.

## Prompt Engineering Approach

The prompt combines:

* Business Context
* Primary Keyword
* Local SEO
* Search Intent
* Blog Structure
* Internal Linking

This approach generates SEO-focused, user-friendly, and business-oriented content.
